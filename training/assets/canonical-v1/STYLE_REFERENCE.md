# Canonical Exercise Illustration Style — V1

Status: APPROVED REFERENCE STYLE

## Visual language
- Clean, sober digital fitness illustration on a pure white background.
- Neutral instructional look; no decorative infographic panels, labels, badges or UI elements inside the image.
- Athletic but realistic adult body proportions; avoid exaggerated bodybuilding anatomy.
- Simple black sports clothing and black shoes; no shirtless / bare-torso subjects.
- Gym equipment in neutral silver/gray/black tones.
- Crisp dark contour lines, restrained cel-style shading, limited highlights, low visual noise.
- Entire subject and relevant equipment visible with generous white margin; nothing important cropped.
- One clear exercise position by default. Use two positions in the same image only when motion cannot be understood reliably from one pose (e.g. deadlift).
- No text inside exercise images.
- Across the catalog, aim for an approximately 50/50 female/male distribution when exercises are added or replaced.

## Canonical generation prompt
Create a high-resolution, clean and sober digital fitness illustration on a pure white background. Show an athletic adult performing [EXERCISE] with anatomically plausible posture and correctly represented equipment. Use the established project style: crisp dark contour lines, restrained cel-style shading, realistic but not hyper-muscular proportions, black sports clothing that covers the torso, black training shoes, and neutral gray/black gym equipment. Keep the full person and all relevant equipment visible with generous white margins. No labels, text, arrows, infographic panels, logos, decorative background, photorealism, or exposed bare torso. Use a single clear pose unless the movement itself needs two positions to be understandable; if so, show start and end positions cleanly in the same image. Maintain the project's female/male balance across the whole catalog.
